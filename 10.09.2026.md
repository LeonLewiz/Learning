### 4. Alertmanager + Telegram

Поставил Alertmanager (порт 9093), настроил `alertmanager.yml` на нативный telegram-ресивер:

```yaml
route:
  receiver: telegram
  group_by: ['alertname']
  group_wait: 30s
  repeat_interval: 1h

receivers:
  - name: telegram
    telegram_configs:
      - bot_token: "{{ токен }}"
        chat_id: 5534301096
        parse_mode: HTML
```
